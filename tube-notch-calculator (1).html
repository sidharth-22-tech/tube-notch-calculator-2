Assumptions
· Cut tube has an equal or smaller OD than the parent tube.
· Tube axes are assumed to intersect (standard saddle joint).
· Joint angle is the acute included angle (90° = straight T).
· Print at 100% / "actual size" for the template to be true to scale.
· Curve geometry cross-checked against an independent 3D solver and a real CAD boolean intersection — exact match to 3 decimal places.
· Near-equal diameters produce a pointed curve (sharp corners), not a smooth wave — this is correct, expected geometry.
